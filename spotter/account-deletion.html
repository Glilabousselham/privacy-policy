Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Spotter: The Social Gym Tracker & Athlete Community
Developer: Bousselham

We value your privacy and believe you should always maintain full control over your personal information. In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users of Spotter: The Social Gym Tracker & Athlete Community can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data from our systems using the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To ensure user privacy and prevent unauthorized requests, please follow these steps:

  1. Identity Verification:

    You must send your request from the same email address associated with your registered Spotter account. Requests received from unrecognized email addresses cannot be processed.

  2. Recipient Address:

    Send the email directly to our support team at:
    glilabousselham01@gmail.com

  3. Email Subject Line:

    Set the subject line exactly to:
    Account Deletion Request - [Insert Username or Registered Email]

  4. Confirmation & Submission:

    Click the button below to automatically generate and pre-fill the deletion request in your default email client:

    ✉ Request Account Deletion via Email

Data Retention & Deletion Breakdown

Once your deletion request is verified and processed, here is how your data will be handled:

✕ What Gets Permanently Deleted

  • User profile details, bio, and identifiers
  • Authentication credentials and login tokens
  • Workout sessions, exercise logs, and timer history
  • Social community posts, reactions, and comments
  • Media uploads (workout photos, athlete videos)
  • Followers, following list, and friend activity

🔒 What May Be Retained

  • Fully aggregated and anonymized telemetry/analytics (cannot be tied back to you)
  • Financial transactions, subscription records, or billing invoices strictly required for legal, tax, or regulatory compliance

Processing Timeline

Processing Window: Requests are verified and completed within 14 to 30 days from the receipt of your email.
Irreversible Action: Once deletion is complete, the process is permanent and cannot be reversed. Account recovery, restoring past workout records, retrieving saved media, or recovering community connections will no longer be possible.
