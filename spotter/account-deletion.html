Account & Data Deletion Policy

Standard operating procedure for requesting the permanent deletion of a user account and associated personal data.

Application Spotter: The Social Gym Tracker & Athlete Community
Developer / Publisher Bousselham
Regulatory Compliance Google Play Account Deletion Policy, GDPR, CCPA

Effective Date September 11, 2026

1. Overview & Purpose

In accordance with the Google Play Account Deletion requirement, users of Spotter: The Social Gym Tracker & Athlete Community have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all personal information associated with their identity.

This document specifies the required verification procedure, details what categories of data are purged or retained, and outlines processing timelines. This page is publicly accessible and does not require active session credentials or account login.

2. Account Deletion Procedure

To ensure identity authenticity and prevent unauthorized account destruction, all requests are executed through direct verification via registered email:

  1. Initiate from Registered Address: The request must originate from the exact email address tied to your registered Spotter account. Requests received from third-party or unverified addresses will be rejected for security.
  2. Recipient Address: Send the deletion request to:
    glilabousselham01@gmail.com
  3. Subject Line: Ensure the subject line follows this exact structure:
    Account Deletion Request - [Insert Username or Registered Email]
  4. Email Body Content: Use the standard template below to provide your account identifiers: 
    To: glilabousselham01@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request - Spotter - [Your Username or Email]

Hello Support,

I am requesting the permanent deletion of my account and all associated personal data from "Spotter: The Social Gym Tracker & Athlete Community".

Account Details:
- Registered Email Address: [Enter your registered email here]
- Username / Athlete Handle: [Enter your username here]

I confirm that I am sending this request from the registered email address and understand that this process is permanent and irreversible.

Thank you.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Specification

Upon verification and execution of the deletion request, user data is classified and processed as follows:

Data Category Items Included Handling & Policy
User Profile Full name, username, bio, avatar picture, birth date, gender, height/weight records Permanently Purged
Erased from primary databases and caches.
Authentication Hashed passwords, active session tokens, OAuth links, email verification records Permanently Purged
All credentials invalidated immediately.
Activity Logs Workout history, routine templates, exercise sets/reps/weights, timer records, PR metrics Permanently Purged
Unrecoverable once execution completes.
Social & Media Community feed posts, comments, likes, workout media (photos/videos), follower and following links Permanently Purged
Removed from feed and storage buckets.
Aggregated Analytics High-level system telemetry, performance counters, crash diagnostics Retained (De-identified)
Fully anonymized; stripped of any individual or device identifiers.
Legal & Financial In-app purchase transaction logs, tax invoices, payment dispute records Retained (Compliance)
Preserved strictly for legally mandated periods under statutory tax regulations.

4. Processing Timeline & Finality

Processing Window

All valid deletion requests are verified, queued, and completed within 14 to 30 business days from receipt of the email confirmation.

Irreversible Action

Account deletion is final and non-reversible. Once the procedure is executed, your workout history, athlete statistics, media, and social profile cannot be restored by support personnel.
